BENNY'S
FROZEN ADVENTURE

Project Overview

A custom 3D game built in Unity where the player attempts to catch falling ice cream scoops and fulfill rapid-fire orders. The goal was to build a highly responsive, physics-based minigame with a polished visual aesthetic.

This was a project made by myself and three other group members for our Interactive Multimedia Design course at Carleton University. I was responsible for the core gameplay systems, physics interactions, and overall game feel.
